Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how the Uplift Register site may use cookies and similar technologies for functionality, analytics, campaign measurement and security.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files and similar technologies that may be stored on a device when a user visits a website. They can help a site function, improve security, remember settings, measure performance and support campaign attribution.

2. How Uplift Register may use cookies and similar technologies

Uplift Register may use cookies or similar technologies for essential site functionality, security and traffic protection, analytics and performance measurement, campaign attribution, LinkedIn advertising, LinkedIn Insight Tag or conversion tracking where used, and waitlist or form functionality where relevant.

3. Third-party services

The site may use third-party service providers including GitHub Pages for hosting, Cloudflare for domain, security and performance services, LinkedIn for advertising or conversion tracking, Waitlist / GetWaitlist or an equivalent waitlist-form provider where used, and email or analytics service providers where applicable.

4. Managing cookies

Users can manage or block cookies through their browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect site functionality, form behaviour or performance measurement.

5. Analytics and advertising choices

Users may also be able to manage LinkedIn advertising preferences through LinkedIn and through browser or device settings, depending on the tools in use.
